Share a stack - OXT Community Stacks

Share a stack you made

Anyone can share a stack here. A person looks at each one before it is listed, usually within a few days.

In short

  1. Check that your stack plays in OXT-Beyond's Stack Player.
  2. In OXT-Beyond, choose File > Share Stack... and answer four questions.
  3. Post the file it makes on the forum. That is all: we do the rest.

What you need

  • Your stack, saved from OXT-Beyond (an .oxtstack, .livecode or .rev file), and any pictures or sounds it loads from files beside it.
  • A title, your name as you want it shown, and a sentence or two about what the stack does.
  • A licence: the terms on which others may use your stack. If you are not sure, MIT lets people use and learn from it freely as long as they keep your name on it.
  • A screenshot, if you can. Stacks with a picture get played far more.

What makes a stack work here

  • It plays in a web browser, so it must work in OXT-Beyond's Stack Player. Try it there first.
  • No password on the stack. Everyone must be able to read a shared stack's scripts.
  • No shell, no starting other programs, no externals of its own. The libraries that come with OXT-Beyond's web engine are fine.
  • Using the internet (put url) and JavaScript is fine. The stack's page will say so.

How to send it

  1. Easiest: from OXT-Beyond. Open your stack and choose File > Share Stack... It asks for the title, your name, what the stack does and a licence, takes the picture for you, and puts one file on your Desktop. Attach that file to a post on the OpenXTalk forum and say you would like it in the community stacks. We will do the rest.
  2. If your File menu has no Share Stack: it came after OXT-Beyond 0.2.8-dp.1. Attach your stack itself to a post on the forum, with its title, your name, what it does and the licence you choose.
  3. If you use GitHub: add a folder stacks/your-stack-name/1.0/ with your stack and a listing.json to the stacks repository, in a pull request. The repository's README shows how. The checker answers on the pull request in plain words.

Share Stack sends nothing by itself. Your stack leaves your computer when you post it, and not before.
